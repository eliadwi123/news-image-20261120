# The World Today — Friday, 20 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Sum up 20 November's world news in one image"**
(`news-image-20261120`).

`main.py` decodes the poster held in `news.b64` and writes it to
`/output/entry.png` — one page that sums up the day: the Trump–Xi–Putin
trilateral summit in Shenzhen and its peace declaration, Russia's
400-drone / 110-missile attack on Ukraine spilling into Poland, the ICJ
ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, tankers hit
in the Strait of Hormuz under a US blockade, the AI-led market sell-off
(S&P 500 and Dow into correction, Nikkei −5.1%) on the day of Anthropic's
record $81B IPO, the 9-hour global Microsoft 365 outage, COP31's fossil-fuel
deadlock in Antalya, and more.
