# The World Today — Friday, 20 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Sum up 20 November's world news in
one image"** (`news-image-20261120`).

`main.py` copies the poster held in `news.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
The poster sums up the day on one page:

- **Lead:** Trump, Xi and Putin hold their first trilateral summit in
  Shenzhen (APEC) and issue a joint peace declaration; Germany's Merz
  rejects a "new Yalta" and Zelensky calls Putin a killer.
- Russia fires 400+ drones and 110 missiles at Ukraine in one of the
  war's biggest attacks — Kyiv's Dnipro bridges hit, projectiles land
  in Poland, a Crimean Bridge span is downed, a bus in Kramatorsk is
  bombed.
- The ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya.
- Tankers are hit in the Strait of Hormuz as the US blockades Iranian
  shipping and talks stall.
- An AI-led sell-off tips the S&P 500 and Dow into correction and the
  Nikkei falls 5.1% — on the day Anthropic's record $81B IPO opens +33%.
- Microsoft 365 is down worldwide for 9+ hours; the US national cyber
  director steps down.
- Also today: COP31 deadlocked over fossil fuels in Antalya, 34 killed
  in Haiti gang violence, Houthi missiles at Riyadh intercepted,
  BepiColombo reaches Mercury orbit, and a trial pill makes pancreatic
  tumours vanish in some patients.

The image is a whole PNG, 1024 × 1024, under the 1 MiB cap.
